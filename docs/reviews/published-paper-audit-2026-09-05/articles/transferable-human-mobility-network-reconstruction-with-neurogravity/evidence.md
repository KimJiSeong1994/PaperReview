# Evidence — Transferable Human Mobility Network Reconstruction with neuroGravity

## Primary source read
- arXiv:2604.23678; official HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2604.23678; journal DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s43588-026-01003-y

## Method anchors
- neuroGravity combines a gravity-model physics prior with learned regional representations to reconstruct flows from facility and population distributions.
- The paper evaluates partial-observation reconstruction, learned embedding associations, cross-city transfer, and income-segregation transferability analysis.

## Result checks
- The primary paper reports production of mobility proxies for more than 1,200 cities and identifies source–target segregation similarity as associated with reconstruction transferability.

## Limits retained in article
- Associations from learned regional embeddings and segregation analyses are observational model analyses, not policy-intervention effects.
- Generalization evidence is tied to the selected cities, available public covariates, and reported transfer splits.
