# Evidence — Quantum machine learning models for graphs

## Primary source read
- arXiv:2607.00698, official HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2607.00698

## Method anchors
- The paper characterizes equivariant quantum graph-model constituents by permutation-symmetry twirling for n-node/n-qubit encodings.
- It treats linear and nonlinear equivariant maps, then studies known-model integration and classical pre-training.

## Result checks
- The numerical sections demonstrate graph discrimination and classical pre-training constructions; they are not hardware or application deployment benchmarks.

## Limits retained in article
- Claims are conditional on the stated n-node/n-qubit encoding and symmetry group; the paper identifies open scalability and implementation questions.
