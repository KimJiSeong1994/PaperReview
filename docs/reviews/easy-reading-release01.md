## Review controls

Each manuscript passed skeptical and factual review, correction checks, the actual im-not-ai Korean style workflow, and an independent post-edit semantic check. Numeric occurrences, links, references and figure descriptions were retained through polishing. PaperWiki-reviewed files are the source of the published body.

Source-specific corrections separate evaluation protocols, benchmark denominators, causal attribution, proxy costs, publication metadata and figure editions. No training experiment was independently reproduced.

IDs, titles, URLs, original publication dates, categories and tags are retained. Unselected posts are unchanged.
