Search integrity review and implementation, 2026-11-19
Base: 1e6456e9a6714ab1ae31be86c31e1f0ec2a2f34a (verified production revision).

Confirmed causes:
1. Title/fuzzy dedup admitted conflicting KCI IDs and disjoint author/year evidence.
   DOI-less records acquired another paper's DOI. Explicit DOI identity remains authoritative.
2. Request filters did not incorporate analyzed time constraints. Category descriptions
   such as "graph machine learning" must not become arXiv category filters.
3. KCI URLs entered topic retrieval, truncating the article ID and matching URL pollution.
4. Chronological KCI timeouts fell through to relevance-selected OpenAlex results.
5. Graph expansion crossed the selected source boundary without explicit request control.
6. Partial-result metadata was not explained in the results UI.

Changes:
Conservative identity matching; KCI URL/ID rowid lookup; grounded natural-language year
constraints with explicit controls taking precedence; effective filter metadata restored
on cache hits; date constraints removed from KCI topic tokens; explicit graph opt-in and
strict-source option; no relevance fallback or partial cache for chronological searches;
partial-result notice; read/ingestion title sanitation and journaled FTS repair utility.
The integrated UI and conversation caller explicitly opt into related-paper expansion.

Limits:

misleading older results. Google Scholar 403 is a provider access limitation, not cured
by these integrity changes. Legacy corpus repair requires the journaled --apply command.
